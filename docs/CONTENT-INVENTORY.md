## Interactions

The export used local state to switch pages, show statistic notes, select history years, change orientation, filter facilities, select clubs and open FAQs. It contained gallery state and timed statistical count-ups. Inscriptions, Recrutement, Personnel and Académie were empty heading-only pages. Contact and newsletter buttons only changed local success text; they did not send data.

The redesign retains useful selectors, makes the gallery explicit, adds requested interactive experiences and replaces fake submission states with official contact links and a pending newsletter notice. Statistical count-ups were omitted because the figures are unverified.
