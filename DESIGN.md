# The Bonaparte yearbook

On this site, Bonaparte's campus, French school life and anniversary archive become a living editorial yearbook.

Audience: families discovering the school and pupils exploring their community. The first screen identifies the school and gives direct entry to its life and practical information.

Creative contract: French editorial authority with the warmth of a student-made annual. Fraunces carries literary display headings; Public Sans carries clear navigation and long-form text. Preserve the original identity's navy and red, with warm paper and occasional album blue. The main composition uses a wide masthead image and a repeatable 42% editorial axis. Vary full-width imagery, broad text spreads, tactile objects and index lists rather than repeated cards.

Signature motif: opening the book of school life. Entrance doors part into the campus masthead, then pages become an actual scrapbook and the media identity becomes a CD sleeve. Fast reversible control transitions; one short entrance per session; no scroll hijacking, custom cursor or autoplay audio. Honor reduced motion.

Asset policy: reuse supplied photography with its original subject association. No generated photographs. Missing trip photos and unverified awards are labeled in their designed slots. src/images.js is the replacement manifest. All original content arrays are preserved in src/content.js.

Taste direction: editorial-report. Retain its wide image, editorial axis, solid-color spreads, table-like directories and oversized footer. Adapt its pinned gallery to a compact campus gallery to preserve easy navigation, and retain accessible FAQ disclosure interactions from the original. Tactile album/disc shadows and CD material gradients are intentional physical object cues, not page decoration.

Build directly from the user's detailed brief and delegated creative authority. No persistent Impeccable build preference is inferred. No fabricated sample facts fill the layout.
